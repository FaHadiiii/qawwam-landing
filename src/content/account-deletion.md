**Effective date:** 2026-08-16 · **Version:** 1.0.0

At Qawwam, we respect your control over your personal data. You may delete your
account and all associated records at any time, directly from within the App.

## 1. How to Delete Your Account

To permanently delete your account:

1. Open the Qawwam app on your device.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Select **My Account**.
4. Tap **Delete My Account** and confirm when prompted.

## 2. What Happens When You Delete

Deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. Your account will be permanently
deleted and all records associated with it will be permanently lost, including:

- Your profile and personal information.
- Your saved preferences, settings, and bookmarks.
- Your Qur'an reading history, saved duas, and any other content synced to your
  account.

Once deleted, your account **cannot be restored** or recovered for any reason.

## 3. Data Retention

Upon deletion, your personal data is removed from our systems. Copies of data
may be retained only to the extent required to comply with legal obligations,
resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, and will be handled in accordance
with our [Privacy Policy](#privacy-policy).

## 4. Before You Delete

Because deletion is permanent, we encourage you to save or note down any content
or preferences you wish to keep before proceeding. We are not able to retrieve
your data after your account has been deleted.

## 5. Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or need help, please contact us
through the support details provided in the App.
