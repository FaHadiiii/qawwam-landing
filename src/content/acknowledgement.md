**Effective date:** 2026-10-25 · **Version:** 1.2.1

Qawwam draws on the work of numerous institutions and individuals to provide
authentic Islamic content. We are grateful for their contribution to the
ummah and acknowledge the following sources.

## 1. Prayer Times

- **Official prayer times** are sourced from the respective national religious
  authorities:
  - Malaysia — JAKIM / Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia
  - Indonesia — Kemenag / Kementerian Agama Republik Indonesia
  - Singapore — MUIS / Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura
  - Brunei — KHEU / Kementerian Hal Ehwal Ugama

## 2. Qur'an

- **Qur'an text** — the Uthmani script is the **Tanzil Project**'s
  ([tanzil.net](https://tanzil.net)), reproduced unmodified under the **Creative
  Commons Attribution 3.0** licence. Tanzil's copyright notice is carried in full
  in the search index.
- **Translations** — English: *Sahih International*; Malay: *Abdullah Basmeih*.
- **Arabic commentary** — *Tafsir Al-Muyassar*, King Fahd Qur'an Printing
  Complex. It is commentary on the verse rather than a translation of it, and is
  presented as commentary in the App.
- **Transliteration** — the *en.transliteration* edition.
- **Printed Mushaf** — the per-page glyph fonts and the word and page layout
  databases come from the **Quranic Universal Library (QUL)**
  ([qul.tarteel.ai](https://qul.tarteel.ai)) and render the **KFGQPC Uthmanic
  script v4** print (1441H) of the King Fahd Qur'an Printing Complex.
- **Search index** — Tanzil Quran Text (Simple, Version 1.1), also under the
  Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence.
- **Recitations** — per-Ayah and whole-Surah audio is streamed from
  [islamic.network](https://islamic.network). Recitations remain the property of
  their respective reciters and publishers, and are reproduced for personal
  devotional use.
- **Reciter portraits** — portraits in the audio picker identify the reciter
  whose recording is offered. They remain the property of their respective
  owners and do not imply endorsement by the reciter or publisher.
- The translation, commentary and transliteration editions above were obtained
  through [alquran.cloud](https://alquran.cloud).

## 3. Recitation Assistance

- On-device recitation assistance and Quran voice search use the open-source
  [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) runtime, with the
  **QuranLab** acoustic model and phoneme reference. These run locally;
  recitation audio is not sent to Qawwam. The model and reference data remain
  subject to the terms under which they are distributed.

## 4. Backgrounds

- **Wallpapers** — photo and video backgrounds are supplied by
  [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) and its contributors under the
  [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Photographer credits are
  shown in the App where available.

## 5. Supplications (Duas) and Names of Allah

- Duas content is self-hosted from islamicapi.com.
- Asma-ul-Husna (the 99 Names of Allah) content is curated from traditional
  sources.

## 6. Our Contribution

- Prayer time aggregation, zone mapping, Hijri date conversion, the Khatam
  reading journeys (solo and group), offline reading sync, the Quran audio
  player, background and ambience controls, notification delivery, application
  logic and API infrastructure are original work by the Qawwam team.
