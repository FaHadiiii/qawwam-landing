# Acknowledgement

**Effective date:** 2026-08-14 · **Version:** 1.0.0

Qawwam draws on the work of numerous institutions and individuals to provide
authentic Islamic content. We are grateful for their contribution to the
ummah and acknowledge the following sources.

## 1. Prayer Times

- **Official prayer times** are sourced from the respective national religious
  authorities:
  - Malaysia — JAKIM / Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia
  - Indonesia — Kemenag / Kementerian Agama Republik Indonesia
  - Singapore — MUIS / Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura
  - Brunei — KHEU / Kementerian Hal Ehwal Ugama
- Where official data is unavailable, prayer times are computed using
  astronomical calculations via the [Adhan](https://github.com/batoulapps/adhan)
  library.

## 2. Qur'an

- Qur'an text, transliteration, translations, and recitations are provided
  through publicly available Qur'an data sources. Audio recitations remain the
  property of their respective reciters and publishers, and are reproduced for
  personal devotional use.

## 3. Supplications (Duas) and Names of Allah

- Duas content is self-hosted from islamicapi.com.
- Asma-ul-Husna (the 99 Names of Allah) content is curated from traditional
  sources.

## 4. Our Contribution

- Prayer time aggregation, zone mapping, Hijri date conversion, application
  logic, and API infrastructure are original work by the Qawwam team.

## 5. Copyright Notice

All Qur'anic text and audio, duas, translations, and prayer data are the

We ask that users recite \`Bismillah\` and remember the contributors in their
duas.
