# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-14 · **Version:** 1.0.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Qawwam ("we", "us") collects, uses, and
protects your information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name
  and email address from your Google profile.
- **Activity data:** Bookmarked surahs and ayahs, notes, reading progress,
  prayer records, fasting records, and similar data you create in the App. This
  data is synced to your account so it is available across your devices.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** Aggregated, non-identifying information about how the App is
  used, such as which endpoints are requested, to help us operate and improve
  the Service.

### 1.3 Location
- When you request prayer times by location, the coordinates you provide are
  used only to calculate prayer times for that request and are not stored.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

- Provide, maintain, and personalize the Service.
- Sync your content across devices via your account.
- Respond to your requests and provide support.
- Improve the App's features and reliability.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 3. Authentication with Google

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. We receive only the profile
information Google shares with us (name, email, and profile picture). We do not
have access to, and never store, your Google password. Google's privacy policy
applies to your use of Google Sign-In.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. We retain your data
for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You
may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by
contacting us.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

- When required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- With service providers (such as Cloudflare) who process data on our behalf
  under confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and correction:** You can view and edit most of your data within the
  App.
- **Deletion:** You may request that we delete your account and personal data.
- **Sync:** You can stop syncing by logging out of the App.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the
"Effective date" above and, where material, notify you through the App.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the contact details
provided in the App.
